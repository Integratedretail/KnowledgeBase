FootfallCam Warranty Terms & Conditions

Last update: 24 August 2026

 

 

1. About This Document​

Integrated Retail Pte Ltd ("Integrated Retail", "we", "us") supplies FootfallCam® people counting hardware and software to customers across Southeast Asia as an authorised reseller. This document sets out the warranty that we provide to you ("the Customer", "you") for FootfallCam hardware purchased from us or from a sub-reseller or installer appointed by us.

The warranty described here mirrors the manufacturer's 12-month hardware warranty and adds the regional service arrangements we operate for Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand and Indonesia — including where you send a faulty unit, who pays for freight, and how long a replacement typically takes.

Where this document conflicts with a signed supply agreement, master services agreement or tender contract between you and Integrated Retail, that signed agreement takes precedence.

Quick summary: FootfallCam devices carry a 12-month hardware warranty covering manufacturing and material defects. A faulty device is replaced with a unit from our local stock — we do not carry out repairs. You return the faulty unit to our office in your own country, so you never handle export or customs. Accessories, consumables, software subscriptions, installation labour and damage from misuse, environment or pests are not covered.

3. Definitions​

Product — a FootfallCam counting device (for example FootfallCam 3D Pro2™, 3D Extend™, 3D ProWave, Pro1 series IP cameras, Pro3 AI Box, Centroid series NVR) supplied by Integrated Retail.

Accessories — items supplied alongside a Product that are not the counting device itself, including mounting brackets, poles, enclosures, power adapters, PoE injectors, network switches, SD cards, cables and connectors.

Warranty Period — the 12-month period described in §4.

Replacement Unit — a unit supplied by Integrated Retail from local stock to replace a device accepted as defective under this warranty.

RMA — Return Merchandise Authorisation; the reference number we issue that authorises you to return a unit for inspection.

Local Office — the Integrated Retail office or authorised receiving facility in your country (Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand or Indonesia) nominated by us to receive returned units.

Support Contract — a paid service subscription that sits alongside this warranty and determines how replacements are handled. Integrated Retail offers Standard Support and Premium Support; the difference relevant to this document is the replacement process (see §9). Eligibility, pricing and full terms are set out in the Support Contract agreement.

** **

4. Standard Hardware Warranty​

Integrated Retail warrants that each Product will be free from defects in materials and workmanship, under normal use and in the operating conditions specified by the manufacturer, for 12 months.

4.1 When the Warranty Period starts​

The Warranty Period begins on the date the device is first registered to the FootfallCam analytics platform (the "first allocation" date recorded by the platform).

If a device is never registered to the platform, the Warranty Period is treated as starting 30 days after the invoice date. Devices held in a customer's own stock are not granted an open-ended warranty start date; please plan deployments accordingly, or ask us about a stock-holding arrangement before you order.

4.2 What we will do​

Integrated Retail does not carry out hardware repairs. We hold replacement stock locally, and a valid claim is resolved by replacement.

Every claim is investigated and troubleshot before any replacement is issued. See §8 for the process and §9.5 for the checks we complete before releasing an advance replacement.

For a valid claim, Integrated Retail will, at our sole option and at no charge to you:

  1. supply a replacement unit of the same specification from our stock; or

  2. where a replacement of that model is unavailable, supply the nearest current equivalent model; or

  3. where neither is commercially practicable, issue a credit against the unit based on its unexpired warranty life.

In some cases we may supply a higher-specification unit as a replacement. This is at our discretion, subject to approval, and does not create an entitlement to an upgrade on any future claim.

The replaced unit becomes the property of Integrated Retail or the manufacturer once received. A replacement unit carries the unexpired balance of the original device's 12-month Warranty Period. Replacement does not restart, extend or add to that period, and does not create a new 12-month warranty on the replacement unit.

4.3 Proof of purchase​

To claim, you must be able to produce:

  • the Integrated Retail invoice, delivery order or sales receipt for the unit; and

  • the device serial number, legible and unaltered; and

  • where extended warranty applies, evidence of payment and the extended warranty schedule for that serial number.

Claims without verifiable proof of purchase and a matching serial number cannot be processed.

4.4 Who can claim​

This warranty is granted to the original purchasing entity. It is not transferable to a third party on resale of the equipment, and it cannot be moved from one unit to another. If your business is restructuring, being acquired or transferring assets to an affiliate, write to us before the transfer and we will consider a reassignment in writing.

5. What Is Covered​

  • Failure of the imaging sensor, processing board, memory, internal power circuitry or network interface arising from a manufacturing or material defect.

  • Firmware faults that prevent the device from operating as documented and that cannot be resolved by a firmware update or reconfiguration.

  • Units that fail commissioning tests on first installation due to a defect present at delivery ("dead on arrival"), provided the claim is raised within 14 days of delivery.

  • Supply of a replacement unit from our local stock, and delivery of that unit to you, for the above.

** **

6. What Is Not Covered​

6.1 Items outside the scope of this warranty​

This warranty covers the FootfallCam counting device only. It does not cover:

  • Accessories, mounting brackets, enclosures, poles and fixings.

  • Power supplies, PoE injectors, adapters, switches, routers, SIM/data services and cabling — whether supplied by us or by you. Where these carry a manufacturer's own warranty, we will pass the claim through on your behalf on a reasonable-endeavours basis.

  • Consumables and wear items, including SD cards and batteries.

  • The FootfallCam software platform, apps, dashboards, APIs and cloud hosting. These are governed by your subscription or licence terms.

  • Installation, de-installation, re-installation, cabling works, ceiling works, scaffolding, lift or scissor-lift hire, site access charges and permits.

  • Data loss, historical count data, report regeneration or any loss of analytics continuity.

  • Third-party products supplied for your convenience but not manufactured by FootfallCam.

6.2 Conditions that void or exclude cover​

This warranty does not apply where, in our reasonable assessment:

  1. Serial number — the serial number or asset label has been altered, defaced, tampered with or removed.

  2. Unauthorised work — the unit has been opened, dismantled, repaired or modified by anyone other than Integrated Retail, an authorised service partner, or the manufacturer. Doing so forfeits the remaining warranty on that unit.

  3. Installation and operation — the unit was not installed, configured, maintained or operated in accordance with the manufacturer's documentation and mounting-height, coverage and network requirements, or was subject to adjustments, alterations or servicing not covered in that documentation and not authorised by us in writing.

  4. Environment — damage caused by conditions outside the specified operating range. Relevant to our region, this includes:

installation outdoors or in dusty, humid, wet or otherwise hostile locations without suitable protective housing;

  1. exposure of the sensor to direct sunlight, halogen light or other intense light sources, whether the device is powered on or not — this can permanently damage the image sensor and applies to indoor as well as outdoor placements;

  2. water ingress, condensation, flooding, monsoon or storm damage;

  3. heat exposure, poor ventilation, or installation directly against air-conditioning outlets or heat-generating fixtures;

electrical surges, lightning strikes, unstable mains supply, brownouts and incorrect voltage. Devices should be powered through a surge-protected, correctly earthed supply. Pests — damage caused by rodents, lizards, geckos, cockroaches, ants, termites or other pests, including cable gnawing and nesting inside enclosures. Pest activity is a common cause of failure in tropical installations and is not a manufacturing defect. We can advise on cable conduit and enclosure options at the design stage. Handling — misuse, abuse, negligence, accident, dropping, vandalism, theft, or damage sustained in transit after the unit has left our custody. Force majeure — earthquake, flood, fire, typhoon, civil unrest, war, or other events beyond reasonable control. Normal wear — cosmetic marks, scratches, discolouration, yellowing of plastics, faded labels and other cosmetic damage that does not affect counting function. No fault found — units returned that test as functioning to specification (see §8.5). Non-authorised supply — units not purchased from Integrated Retail, from a reseller or installer we have appointed, or from another FootfallCam authorised channel.** **

This warranty is given in addition to, and does not limit or replace, any rights you may have under the laws applicable in the country where you purchased the Product. 8. How To Make A Warranty Claim (RMA Process)​

Step 1 — Raise a support ticket​

Log the issue with Integrated Retail at connect@integratedretail.com, or through the FootfallCam support portal if your account is set up for direct access. Include:

  • device serial number(s);

  • site name and address;

  • invoice or delivery order number;

  • a description of the fault, when it started, and what has changed on site;

  • screenshots of the device status page or platform error, and photographs of the installed unit and its cabling where relevant.

Step 2 — Remote diagnosis​

Our engineers will attempt remote diagnosis first. A large share of reported faults are resolved without a return — typically network, PoE, firmware, mounting-height or configuration issues. You agree to provide reasonable co-operation, including remote access to the device or network where required, and to carry out basic on-site checks we request (power cycling, cable swap, port change).

Target first response: 24 hours during business hours, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in the relevant country.

Step 3 — RMA issued​

If remote diagnosis indicates a hardware fault, we issue an RMA number and the shipping address of the Local Office for your country. Do not ship any unit without an RMA number — unauthorised returns may be refused or delayed.

Step 4 — Pack and ship​

  • Return the counting device only, unless we specifically request accessories.

  • Use the original packaging where possible, or equivalent anti-static and shock protection. Damage in transit due to inadequate packing is not covered.

  • Mark the RMA number clearly on the outside of the carton and on the enclosed paperwork.

  • Enclose the paperwork we issue with the RMA. As the return is domestic to our local office, no export declaration or commercial invoice is needed from you.

Step 5 — Inspection and outcome​

We inspect and test the unit and confirm one of the following:

  • In-warranty defect — replaced at no charge; outbound freight at our cost.

  • Out of warranty or excluded — we quote for a replacement unit at the prevailing commercial price. Nothing is supplied or invoiced without your written approval. The faulty unit is returned to you at your cost or, with your consent, disposed of after 60 days.

  • No fault found — see §8.5.

Step 6 — Return and re-commissioning​

The unit is dispatched to your nominated address in-country. Re-installation and re-commissioning on site are your responsibility unless you hold an on-site service cover or purchase installation separately.

8.2 Freight, duties and clearance​

All warranty returns are domestic. You ship the faulty unit to the Integrated Retail office in your own country — Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand or Indonesia. You do not export the device, deal with customs, or pay any import duty.

Where a unit needs to go to the manufacturer, Integrated Retail handles that leg. Cross-border freight, export and import documentation, duties and clearance between our office and FootfallCam are arranged and paid for by us, and are not passed on to you.

  • Inbound freight to our local office: your cost and risk. Domestic courier only.

  • Outbound freight for a valid in-warranty replacement: our cost, to one nominated address per RMA within your country.

  • Cross-border freight, duties, taxes and customs clearance: our responsibility.

  • Risk: passes to us on receipt at our Local Office, and back to you on dispatch.

8.3 Where to send returns​

Please contact the Integrated Retail team at connect@integratedretail.com for the address and shipping details that apply to your claim.

Always use the address issued with your RMA and quote the RMA number on the carton. Do not ship directly to FootfallCam or to any overseas address — units sent outside this process may be delayed, refused, or held at customs at your cost.

8.4 Data on returned devices​

FootfallCam devices are designed to process imagery on-device and to transmit count data rather than video. Even so, you are responsible for removing any site-specific configuration, credentials or storage media you do not wish to send. We are not liable for loss of data, recordings or configuration on returned units, and we may factory-reset or reimage any unit we receive.

8.5 No-fault-found and handling charges​

If a returned unit tests as operating to specification, we may charge a no-fault-found inspection fee of USD 90 per device plus return freight. We will always tell you before charging. Repeated no-fault-found returns from the same site usually point to a network, power or mounting issue, and we would rather help you fix the root cause — ask us for a site review.

Without an active Support Contract, the full 12-month warranty still applies and claims follow the return-first process in §9.1. Advance replacement is not available.

9.1 Standard Support Contract — return first, then replace​

This is the default process under Standard Support. You return the faulty device to our Local Office, we check and confirm the fault, and we then dispatch a replacement unit from local stock. The device is off-line while it is in transit and under inspection.

9.2 Premium Support Contract — advance replacement included​

Advance replacement is part of the Premium Support contract, at no extra charge and with no deposit. Once we approve the claim, we dispatch a replacement from local stock straight away, before we have received the faulty unit. You then return the faulty device to us under §9.4.

If continuity of counting data matters to you — flagship stores, sites under a landlord footfall-reporting obligation, or anywhere a data gap is costly — Premium Support is the option designed for that.

9.3 Standard Support Contract — advance replacement against a deposit​

Standard Support customers who need a replacement before returning the faulty unit can request advance replacement on a per-claim basis. We ask for a deposit equal to the latest list price of the replacement unit.

The deposit is fully refundable, provided the faulty unit is returned within the window in §9.4. It is released in full once the faulty device has been received back at our local office. The deposit is what allows us to release hardware before we have the faulty unit in hand, so it is held until that unit reaches us — not merely until the replacement reaches you.

To arrange the deposit, or to query a refund, email connect@integratedretail.com.

9.4 Returning the faulty unit (advance replacement only)​

This section applies where we have shipped a replacement before receiving your faulty device — that is, to all Premium Support claims and to Standard Support claims where a deposit-backed advance replacement was granted. It does not apply to the standard return-first process in §9.1, where we already hold the unit.

Advance replacement is a swap, not an additional unit. Once we approve a warranty replacement, you must return the faulty device to our local office within 30 days of the approval date, and in no event later than 60 days.

Why this deadline exists. Warranty cover is finite, and a returned device has to be processed while cover on that serial number is still live. If a claim is approved in month 10 of the warranty period and the faulty unit only reaches us six or twelve months later, the warranty on that device has long expired and the return can no longer be processed as a warranty claim. The 30-day window keeps your claim inside the period it was approved under.

If the faulty unit has not reached us within 60 days of approval, your entitlement to a free-of-charge replacement lapses and the replacement unit in your possession becomes chargeable. How that is settled depends on your contract:

  • Standard Support — the deposit is forfeited and applied as payment for the replacement unit. No further invoice is raised, and no balance is refunded.

  • Premium Support — no deposit was taken, so we will invoice you for the replacement unit at the prevailing price.

In both cases the warranty on the original serial number is closed, and the replacement unit is treated as a normal commercial purchase.

We will send a reminder before the deadline. If a genuine logistical obstacle is holding up the return — a site closure, a landlord access issue, a tenancy handover — tell us before day 60. We will put the request to FootfallCam, though any extension is granted at the manufacturer's discretion and must be confirmed in writing before it takes effect.

9.5 Checks before an advance replacement is released​

Advance replacement means hardware leaves our stock before we have seen the faulty unit, so we complete a series of checks first. These typically include remote diagnostics, a review of the device's platform history, photographs of the installation, and questions about site conditions, power and recent works.

If those checks indicate the fault falls under an exclusion in §6 — pest damage, surge or lightning, water ingress, unauthorised work, or physical damage — no advance replacement is issued. We will tell you what we found and, if you want the unit replaced, quote for a replacement at the prevailing commercial price.

This protects you as much as us: it avoids a situation where you receive a replacement, return the faulty unit, and then face an unexpected invoice once the exclusion comes to light.

10. Other Service Options​

  • Extended warranty — additional 12 or 24 months of the cover described in this document, purchased at or before the point of sale.

Eligible as standard: FootfallCam Pro2 and Pro3 series devices.

  • All other models: extended warranty is not offered as standard. You may submit a special request through your account manager, which we will put to the manufacturer. Approval is not guaranteed, and availability, price and term are confirmed case by case before any order is placed.

  • Extended cover is subject to the terms in this document and to your extended warranty schedule. It lengthens the warranty period only; it does not change what is covered, the exclusions in §6, or your support contract tier.

On-site service — engineer attendance for diagnosis, swap-out and re-commissioning. Availability, response windows and travel charges vary by city and country; coverage outside major metropolitan areas is by quotation.

Speak to your account manager for pricing and availability in your country.
