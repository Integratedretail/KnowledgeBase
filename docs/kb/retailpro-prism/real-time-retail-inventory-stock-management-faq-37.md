# Retail Pro Prism: Real-Time Retail Inventory & Stock Management FAQ

## How does Integrated Retail ensure absolute stock visibility across all store locations?

We utilize **Retail Pro Prism’s** advanced architecture to provide near-real-time, bidirectional data synchronization between your headquarters and every store server. This ensures that your headquarters remains the definitive authority for reporting accurate on-hand quantities across the entire enterprise. Beyond simple stock counts, Retail Pro Prism stock visibility includes a "True On-Hand" feature that accounts for items currently committed to customer orders or incoming transfers, giving you a comprehensive view of what is truly available to sell.

## Can my store staff see live stock levels at other branches?

Yes, our real-time retail inventory solutions empower your sales associates to look up item quantities at any other store within your subsidiary directly from the **Retail Pro Prism** Point of Sale. If an item is out of stock locally, your team can quickly identify which nearby locations have the product, preventing lost sales and improving the customer experience.

## How can we optimize our receiving process to get merchandise on the floor faster?

Integrated Retail specializes in streamlining the receiving workflow within **Retail Pro Prism** through the use of Advanced Shipping Notices (ASNs) and Batch Receiving. ASNs act as electronic manifests that detail exactly what is arriving, allowing your team to verify shipments against original purchase orders with a single click. For high-volume operations, Batch Receiving allows staff to scan entire cartons or pallets at once, automatically updating your real-time retail inventory without the need for manual item-by-item counts.

## What are the benefits of your retail stock transfer solutions?

Our retail stock transfer solutions move merchandise efficiently between locations to meet demand. **Retail Pro Prism** allows you to plan these movements using Transfer Orders and execute them with Transfer Slips, which automatically decrement stock at the source while creating an expected delivery notice at the target store. The system even tracks "in-transit" status and estimated arrival dates, so your managers know exactly when to expect new stock.

## How does the system maintain inventory accuracy and valuation?

Accuracy is maintained through a combination of automated tracking and robust auditing tools in **Retail Pro Prism**. Every movement—from a sale to a return or a stock adjustment—is recorded on transactional documents that update your inventory history. To ensure financial integrity, we configure costing methods that automatically update your inventory valuation based on the latest vendor costs, providing a clear and audited paper trail for your accounting team.

## Can we manage stock across different business units or countries?

Absolutely. We support complex multi-subsidiary transfers within **Retail Pro Prism**, allowing you to move stock between different business entities or international regions. The system automatically handles currency conversions and assigns items to the correct local inventory records. This global scalability is a hallmark of our Integrated Retail inventory management expertise.
