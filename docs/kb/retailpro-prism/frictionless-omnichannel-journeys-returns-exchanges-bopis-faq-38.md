In today's retail landscape, customers expect a unified experience across every touchpoint. **Integrated Retail customer loyalty** is built on providing a "frictionless" journey, whether a customer is buying online and picking up in-store (BOPIS) or returning a gift at a different location from where it was purchased.

Below is an FAQ exploring how our modern retail POS solutions, powered by **Retail Pro Prism**, manage these complex scenarios.

# Frictionless Omnichannel Journeys: Returns, Exchanges & BOPIS FAQ

## How does Integrated Retail support a unified experience across multiple stores?

We provide comprehensive **Retail Pro Prism omnichannel support** by utilizing a centralized architecture that synchronizes data in near-real-time across your entire enterprise. This ensures that customer profiles, inventory levels, and transaction histories are consistent at every location. By using "Centrals" functionality, your staff can perform real-time lookups for customers and gift cards, ensuring the most up-to-date information is always available at the Point of Sale.

## How are seamless return and exchange workflows handled if a customer visits a different store?

**Retail Pro Prism** makes seamless return and exchange workflows a reality through Central Returns. Even if a customer brings a receipt from Store A to Store B, the system can search the central database to find and validate the original transaction.

- **Linked Returns:** Associates can link a return directly to the original sale to ensure the customer is refunded the correct price and that the same original tender is used.

- **Even Exchanges:** Our modern retail POS solutions allow associates to combine sale and return items on a single document, facilitating a smooth "even exchange" in one quick transaction.

## Can we offer "Buy Online, Pick Up In-Store" (BOPIS) and "Send Sale" options?

Yes. **Retail Pro Prism** provides extreme flexibility for order fulfillment. Through the "Send Sale" workflow, an associate at a store that is out of stock can complete a sale and automatically send the fulfillment request to another branch that has the item. The customer can then choose to have the item shipped to them or pick it up themselves at the second location. Additionally, orders can be fulfilled at any store in your network, regardless of where the order was originally placed.

## How does Integrated Retail ensure customer data is consistent for loyalty programs?

Maintaining **Integrated Retail customer loyalty** requires a 360-degree view of the customer. **Retail Pro Prism** replicates key customer data—including purchase history and loyalty point balances—across all your installations. When a customer walks into any of your stores, the associate can instantly see their lifetime value, preferred styles, and available rewards, allowing for a highly personalized shopping experience.

## What happens if a store loses its internet connection during a return or order pickup?

Our modern retail POS solutions include robust "resiliency" features. If a store goes offline, the system gracefully switches to a local mode, allowing associates to continue performing essential tasks like customer lookups and credit redemptions using the last-known data. Once the connection is restored, the system automatically synchronizes the offline transactions with the central server to ensure all records are updated.

## How can we personalize the experience for our high-value VIP customers?

Beyond standard data, **Retail Pro Prism** allows you to capture extensive customer preferences using customizable fields. You can track details like favorite brands or designers, and even add a customer’s image to their profile for visual confirmation at the Point of Sale. This ensures that your most loyal customers receive the white-glove service they expect at every branch.
