# Secure Retail IT Support: Data Sync & Offline Reliability FAQ

## How does the Retail Pro Prism offline POS ensure my business stays operational during an internet outage?

**Retail Pro Prism** is engineered for uninterrupted operations, supporting distributed store activity even during connectivity interruptions. The system includes a graceful offline mode that allows cashiers to continue performing essential tasks like sales and customer lookups using local data. Furthermore, EFT Resiliency allows your stores to continue processing card transactions offline under predefined limits, automatically finalizing them once the connection is restored.

## How reliable is the POS data synchronization between my stores and headquarters?

We utilize a near-real-time, bidirectional **POS data synchronization** model that keeps your entire enterprise in sync. This architecture ensures that Headquarters remains the central authority for master data—such as pricing, promotions, and inventory—while transactional data from stores flows upstream for immediate reporting. The data is delivered via an asynchronous, fault-tolerant messaging backbone that guarantees delivery even if there are temporary network gaps.

## How does Integrated Retail system security protect our sensitive business information?

**Integrated Retail system security** relies on a sophisticated role-based access control system. This allows your **Retail Consultant** to help you define specific employee groups (e.g., Cashiers, Managers, Buyers) where users are granted access only to the features necessary for their specific jobs, following the principle of least-privilege. Sensitive data, such as employee passwords, are protected using industry-standard SHA256 hashing to ensure maximum security.

## Can we customize what our staff can see and do at the Point of Sale?

Yes. Prism provides highly granular security permissions for almost every action at the POS. For tasks that are normally restricted, such as large manual discounts or price changes, the system supports an "override" capability. This allows a manager to temporarily log in to authorize a specific task before the system automatically returns to the original user's restricted session.

## How does the system handle data integrity when a store comes back online?

The system's resiliency services run automatically in the background to monitor connection status. As soon as a connection is re-established, the system initiates an automatic catch-up process, sending all offline transactions and updates to the central server to ensure your records are perfectly aligned. This prevents data discrepancies and reduces the operational risk often associated with manual data entry after an outage.

## What measures are in place to prevent unauthorized access to our workstations?

To further bolster **Integrated Retail system security**, we configure technical safeguards such as automatic workstation locking after a period of inactivity. Additionally, we can enforce strong password policies, requiring complex characters and regular password rotations to prevent unauthorized logins. For certain hardware-related communications, the system utilizes SSL certificates to ensure that data moving between your local devices and the server is encrypted and secure.
